Mentions: Typst

08 Nov 2026 | #mentions

It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a man in possession of a passable manuscript, must be in want of an easy to use and powerful typesetting system. Like Typst.

I needed to create some stylish, legible, print-friendly PDFs for a TTRPG jam1, but I did not have the time to get good at using graphics design tools. Being a software engineer, I decided to spend time learning a new markup language instead. Fortunately, the documentation was easy to digest, the language more familiar looking than LaTeX2, and typst watch lets you setup a live preview easily, providing for a quick onboarding and development feedback loop.

I was pleased with the process and result, and would use it again in the future.

A few simple examples are available in my TTRPG templates Github repository.

  1. The TripTech2 Jam hosted by LionHeart Games, is a game jam where you create a tri-fold pamphlet module for the Mothership TTRPG. My submission, The Seven Sigma Hotel, is free to download. ↩︎

  2. In spite of me previously using LaTeX for reports and such in my university days. ↩︎
